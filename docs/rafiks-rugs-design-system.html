03 · Proposed logo set

Four directions explored.

Concepts test elegance, personality, textile cues, and recognition at ecommerce scale.

A · Expressive wordmarks

Script and ornamental serif routes feel crafted and distinctive, but lose clarity faster at navigation and mobile sizes.

B · Symbol-led routes

Woven letterforms create a direct rug cue. Mirrored RR monogram offers strongest balance of craft, legibility, and scalable recognition.

Selected direction: mirrored RR monogram with tassel terminals, supported by Rafik’s Rugs wordmark and standard/white color variants.
Logo exploration04

04 · Identity

New logo: crafted, mirrored, memorable.

Design idea

Mirrored “R” forms create a balanced monogram. Tassel terminals connect mark directly to rugs and textile craft. Copper adds warmth; high-contrast companion color keeps mark legible.

Variants

  • Standard: light or paper backgrounds.
  • White: dark, photographic, or copper backgrounds.

Usage rules

  • Use supplied files without inversion or recoloring.
  • Keep aspect ratio; never stretch or compress.
  • Allow clear space equal to one tassel width.
  • Use monogram-led scale in header; full lockup in loader and footer.
  • Avoid busy imagery without dark contrast layer.

07 · Experience rules

Movement supports orientation.

Motion

  • Loader progresses once, then clears content.
  • Hero copy enters in a short stagger.
  • Sections reveal on first viewport entry.
  • Cards lift subtly on precise pointers.
  • Reduced-motion preference removes animation.

Responsive behavior

  • Desktop grid becomes two columns, then one.
  • Mobile section rhythm stays compact.
  • Category discovery moves horizontally.
  • Panels own touch scrolling.
  • Checkout summary collapses on small screens.
System intent: preserve memorable brand expression while keeping shopping obvious, accessible, and fast. New features should reuse tokens and components before introducing new visual treatments.
